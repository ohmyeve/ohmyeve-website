About Us

Meet Valery — The Face Behind the Magic

Oh My Eve! is a mobile glitter bar experience bringing a touch of sparkle, joy, and magic to celebrations across Orlando and beyond.

We create unforgettable moments with glitter tattoos, face gems, and beautiful braided hairstyles — all designed to make every guest feel special and full of glow.

All cosmetics used are professional-grade and 100% skin safe.

Our team provides a complete professional setup with high-quality materials, beautiful styling, and a vibrant atmosphere that keeps guests engaged and entertained throughout your event.
